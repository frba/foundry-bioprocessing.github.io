Work With Us

Tell us what you're trying to discover, build, test, automate, or scale. Whether you're exploring a new idea, developing a workflow, or looking for access to specialized capabilities, we can help identify the right next steps.

Starting Points ↓ Ways to Work With Us ↓ Get in Touch ↓

Possible Starting Points

“We need to build or modify a biological system.”

Construct design, DNA assembly, microbial or mammalian engineering, variant generation, and cell-based workflows.

“We need to test, screen, or characterize something.”

Assays, phenotyping, analytical workflows, sequencing preparation, HPLC, mass spectrometry, and microscopy-supported studies.

“We need to automate or scale a workflow.”

Liquid handling, assay miniaturization, workflow optimization, high-throughput implementation, and automation-enabled process development.

“We need access, training, or technical support.”

Research services, collaborative projects, platform access, hosted arrangements, training, and staff-assisted technical work.

What our users are building with us

Researchers and companies work with us in different ways — from hosted access and direct instrument use to services and collaborative projects.

WAYS TO ENGAGE

Different projects need different ways to work together.

The right engagement model depends on the project goals, required capabilities, team involvement, timeline, and level of platform access.

These models are flexible and may be combined or adapted around the project.

Research Services

  • Protocol onboarding, automation & execution
  • Defined milestones and deliverables

Research Collaborations

  • Co-developed research projects.
  • Joint funding applications, incl. Mitacs.
  • Concordia PI expertise, staffing & supervision..

Direct & Hosted Access

  • Reserve instruments
  • Run your own experiments
  • Training, workflows & technical support

Partnership Pathways

  • Dedicated bench &/or office space​.
  • Ideal for startup & project teams with ongoing platform needs.
  • Access to expertise within Concordia’s research ecosystem​.

Important considerations:
Commercial intent
For projects with commercial intent, clients and partners are responsible for confirming material rights, licensing requirements, and freedom-to-operate considerations for their intended use.
·
Public funding
Funding and support models vary by project. Depending on eligibility, project structure, partner type, and available programs, different engagement pathways may be supported through public funding, institutional programs, partner contributions, or other funding mechanisms.

Not sure which model fits? Tell us about the project and we’ll help identify the right approach.

What happens next

After you contact us, someone from business development or the relevant platform team may suggest a short intro call.

1

Review

We review your project goals, materials, timelines, and technical requirements.

2

Route

We identify the relevant platform capabilities, access model, and possible project pathway.

3

Protect

If needed, we can arrange an NDA before discussing confidential details.

4

Scope

We prepare a scope, cost estimate, and next-step plan for review.

For external and commercial projects

We maintain confidentiality, support appropriate intellectual property arrangements.
